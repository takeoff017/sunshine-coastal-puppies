Common questions

How does delivery or pickup work?

We offer both local pickup and safe delivery options to make bringing you new puppy home as convinient as possible

Once your puppy is ready to go home, we'll cordinate a pickup appointment or discuss on the best delivery option based on your location. Every puppy is thoroughly health-checked before depature, and we'll provide all necessary health records, vaccination information, and care instructions

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. a non-refundable deposit is required to reserve a puppy.

The deposit ensures your selected puppy is held exclusively for you while final arrangements are made. The remaining balance is due before pickup or delivery unless otherwise agreed upon.

If you have questions about our reservation process or payment schedule, we're happy to walk you through every step.

What health guarantee do you offer?

Every puppy receives a comprehensive veterinary health examination before joining their new family

Our puppies are up to date on age-appropriate vaccinations and deworming and come with detailed health records. We also provide a health guarantee covering congenital or hereditary conditions, giving you added confidence and peace of mind.

We're commited to responsible breeding practices and remain available to answer questions and provide ongoing support after you puppy goes home.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept several secure payment methods for your convinient, including:

  • Credit & Debit Cards
  • Bank Transfer
  • Cash (for local pickup, when applicable)
  • Gift Cards
  • Other aproved electronic payment methods

    • If you have questions about payment options or scheduling payments, please contact us before placing your deposit.
